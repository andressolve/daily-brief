Read: The more plausible story behind today's late reversal is the bond market, not the data. That points to the same debt-supply/duration anxiety that drove Tuesday's 30Y record continuing to work against the "goldilocks" read this file logged this morning. This is a case where the morning's framing needs real correction, not just an update: calling this "the cleanest growth-and-inflation-positive session this file has tracked" was accurate for the data prints themselves, but the market's actual verdict by the close was closer to "yields matter more than the data today." Bull case: one session's late-day reversal on light end-of-quarter positioning shouldn't be overread; Thursday's open is the better test of whether this is a trend or quarter-end noise.
