AI Ecosystem — NVIDIA Q3 FY27

Delta since this morning: the print landed, and it resolved three of the week's four open questions decisively bullish. AI analyst source reviewed: research/2026/2026-11-17-update.md (report date 2026-11-17) — unchanged from this morning; no newer upstream report expected until tomorrow.

The beat: revenue $115.84B vs. $109.10B consensus (+6%); adjusted EPS $2.68 vs. $2.48 expected; data center revenue $107.6B vs. $101.87B estimate; non-GAAP gross margin 74.9% vs. 74% estimate. Buybacks of $28.1B this quarter, against the $150B plan announced in September.

The guide, raised not just reaffirmed: interim CEO Colette Kress raised FY2028 growth guidance to 75% year-over-year, from the ~70% this file's standing P035 test was pre-set against — a stronger resolution than the test anticipated. Q4 revenue guidance of ~$130B (~90% YoY) also beat the $125.1B estimate. Kress: "We felt very confident, therefore, that we could establish that we will grow 75% year over year in terms of fiscal year 28," citing "data center readiness, the strength and durability of end customer demand, and our customers['] access to capital."

Succession, closed in the plainest language yet: board chair Harvey Jones: "The Nvidia board unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO." Huang's own statement left the door rhetorically open — "If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest... I would be honored to answer these calls" — but the board's language was unambiguous. The board has hired Heidrick & Struggles to run the permanent-CEO search, a primary confirmation this file didn't have this morning.

China/BIS rule, mooted: Nvidia said explicitly it is "not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China" in its outlook — this morning's confirmed BIS export-control rule has no incremental bite on guidance that was already priced at zero.

Still unverified: tonight's coverage found no mention of the separately-reported board "National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee" or the 50%-China-leasing sales bar. Both remain unverified leads, not facts.
