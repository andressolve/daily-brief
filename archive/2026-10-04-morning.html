Iran / Yemen Yemen front activates; US messaging incoherent

Delta since Saturday morning Yemen fighting intensified from "planned" to active combat. Trump non-denial of Camp David + a dismissive public "ready to fold up" rally line. 24-day streak with no Iranian missile launch. Rial pauses its slide.

Yemeni government forces claim 257 precision operations Saturday (~700 Houthi casualties) and 97 more Sunday on the Tor al-Baha/Taiz axis, repelling what they describe as the Houthis' largest, most coordinated offensive against government-held territory in years (Al Jazeera). The Houthis retaliated by claiming a ballistic-missile/drone strike on an Aramco facility near Riyadh causing "large fires" — Saudi coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki dismissed the claim as "misleading," accusing the Houthis of "attempting to divert attention from the scale of the losses and the growing public anger against them" (Al Jazeera). This is a materially different category of event than Friday's "offensive planned for the coming weeks" framing — real, sustained ground combat is happening in Taiz right now.

Treat the casualty numbers as contested, not confirmed: the Yemeni government's self-reported ~700 Houthi casualties is more than 20 times an independent Arab News estimate of ~30 killed in roughly the same 24-hour window. Self-reported body counts from a combatant belong in the same bucket as the recycled-headline and Polymarket-contamination problems this file has flagged before — treat as propaganda-inflated until corroborated. The structural fact that survives the dispute either way: this is active combat, not planning.

Separately, Trump did not deny the Camp David meeting when asked directly — "If I told you, you would have a major story" (Gulf News) — a non-denial, not a confirmation. That sits in notable tension with his own Friday-night rally line that Iran is "ready to fold up" and the US would win "right after the election... maybe before" (GlobalSecurity.org) — a far more dismissive, confident public register than the private NSC session's reported "considering resuming major combat operations" framing. Cross-checked against Saturday's single-source "Iran preparing for major fighting" claim: this file finds 24 consecutive days with no Iranian ballistic missile launch — the longest gap of the resumed war — the more falsifiable, current-state fact, cutting against that claim rather than confirming it. Rial: ~2.686 million rials/$, down slightly (~0.3%) day-over-day — a pause, not a reversal, in the depreciation trend (Pashizi).

Read: Two matched, but opposite-signed, signals this weekend. On the escalation side: real ground combat in Yemen, a claimed strike toward the Saudi capital, and a US president who won't deny a secret strike-planning meeting. On the restraint side: a 24-day missile-free streak from Iran itself, and a president whose public rhetoric keeps implying the US doesn't need to escalate to win. Bear case: two matched escalation signals in two days — the non-denial, and a Houthi claim of reaching toward Riyadh itself — is a worse picture than Saturday's "process, not yet action" framing suggested, regardless of whether the Riyadh claim is literally true. Bull case: the missile-free streak is a real, measurable fact about Iran's own current restraint that outweighs one anonymous "major fighting" claim, and a president who sounds confident of winning without acting is less likely to order new strikes than one who sounds cornered.
