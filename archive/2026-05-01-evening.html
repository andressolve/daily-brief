Iran / Middle East Geopolitics Active War Day 63

Delta since morning: Trump rejected Iran's proposal but left channel open. Classic Trump negotiation: reject, maintain pressure, wait. Scenario weights unchanged at 48/22/13/12/5. The stalemate thesis held through the day.
"They want to make a deal, but I'm not satisfied with it. They're asking for things that I can't agree to. They have made strides, but I am not sure they will ever get there." — President Trump, Oval Office, May 1 2026 (WaPo)

The structural read is unchanged. The sequencing impasse remains the core barrier: Iran wants to resolve Hormuz/blockade before touching nuclear; the US wants nuclear concessions as the price of blockade relief. Neither can accept the other's ordering without losing their primary leverage. Trump's comment — "things I can't agree to" — almost certainly refers to this sequencing demand.

Why didn't Trump escalate? Three reasons: (1) He acknowledged "strides" — there's real substance in the channel he doesn't want to discard. (2) The War Powers "terminated" framing gives him unlimited runway to wait. (3) Oil is falling, which reduces political pressure from gas prices. There's no urgency to bomb when oil is going your direction.
