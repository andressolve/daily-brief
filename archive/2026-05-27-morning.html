Why Iran Leaked It

The leak is a negotiating tactic, not a security breach. By publicizing draft terms, Iran: (1) boxes in IRGC hardliners who might reject terms — harder to oppose once public; (2) creates market momentum (oil crash) that demonstrates the economic incentive for a deal; (3) forces the US hand — denial makes America look like the obstacle; (4) tests domestic and international reaction to specific terms.

The White House "fabrication" denial is also tactical. If the document were entirely fake, the response would be "these aren't even close to what's being discussed." Instead, CBS confirmed key contours from independent diplomatic sources. The most likely truth: the document is a real working draft that Iran published without US consent, and the WH is denying it to maintain negotiating leverage.

Trump's positioning: Vowed to strike a deal but said he's "not yet satisfied with terms" and is willing to resume fighting. This is positioning, not rejection — he wants better terms, not to walk away. Rubio framed remaining gaps as "disagreements over a word, a sentence."
