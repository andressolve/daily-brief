Iran / Middle East

Delta since this morning: Hormuz held flat despite Trump publicly rejecting Iran's reparations demand and ordering a US compensation counter-claim; no material Gaza movement.

The Hormuz contract's flatness is itself the story tonight. Direct gamma-api pulls at 09:00 PT and 17:14 PT both read 46.5% Yes / 53.5% No — zero net movement across the full session, on real volume, not thin trading. This is the first time this cycle a move as large as this morning's -9.0pp has produced no same-day follow-through in either direction.

Trump publicly rejected Iran's reparations demand today — scoffing at the ask and ordering compensation "for all of the people that they have killed," effectively flipping the reparations question back onto Iran. This is a genuine, dated hardening on the US side, layered on top of this morning's Rubio/SNSC exchange. The market's flat reaction suggests it had already priced a hard US "no" on reparations specifically — always the least plausible item on Iran's list to begin with, not a structural update to the broader normalization odds.

Separately, Iran's IRGC spokesman said today that reopening the strait is "not contingent" on the Iran-Oman negotiating track and depends instead on the US fully accepting Iran's conditions — a mild incremental hardening of the "opening is far off" framing, consistent with (not a change from) this morning's read. Foreign Minister Araghchi maintained the Iran-Oman track itself is in its "final stages," a genuine tension within Iran's own public messaging that hasn't resolved.

Gaza: no material Monday-specific development. Netanyahu's formal rejection of the 15-point roadmap (dated Sunday 8/9) remains the standing state; no new Trump response or Hamas move found today beyond what's already logged.
