Iran / Middle East — see state/iran.md

Delta since this morning Retaliation pattern confirmed (Qatar/Bahrain/Kuwait, not UAE) — but Qatar's mediator role is now visibly straining.

Fresh Iranian drone/missile strikes hit Qatar (an early-warning system), Bahrain (fuel tanks), and Kuwait (a Patriot interceptor system — one person wounded by falling debris after Kuwait shot down three ballistic missiles, a cruise missile, and ten drones). US forces struck roughly 90 Iranian targets overnight. This is the same Bahrain/Kuwait/Qatar pattern flagged this morning, now with operational detail rather than a new UAE strike.

The evening's real story: Qatar condemned the strikes and summoned Iran's deputy ambassador in Doha. This morning's brief noted Witkoff and Kushner were running indirect diplomacy through Qatar's PM/FM. A mediator that has just been struck by the other side, and is now publicly condemning it, is a materially weaker channel than the morning brief assumed — this is the kind of structural fact that matters more than any single day's headline.

"If you strike, you'll get hit." — Iranian parliamentary speaker Mohammad Bagher Qalibaf, 7/9. No de-escalation signal from the legislative-hardline layer.

Separately, IRGC naval forces warned that US "interference in determining shipping routes" through Hormuz would draw a "forceful response" and could "seriously disrupt the gradual reopening" of the strait — notable because Iran's own framing describes Hormuz as being in a reopening process, in tension with the tanker-count-based "effectively closed" read this file has been carrying. Both facts should be held simultaneously: transit volume is still near-zero, but Iran's rhetorical frame is about controlling the terms of reopening, not announcing an indefinite closure.

Minor timeline correction to this morning's correction: Iran's official readout states Khamenei's body was laid to rest at the Imam Reza shrine in the early hours of July 10, not completed on 7/9 as this morning's entry had it — the funeral procession ran 7/9, interment crossed midnight. This doesn't change the substance of the correction; it is still Khamenei's own funeral, not a burial of commanders.

Bear case: a strained Qatar channel is a real degradation of the only active off-ramp in this war. If Doha steps back from mediation — even informally, even short of a public rupture — there is no obvious replacement intermediary, and the war's most likely path to any negotiated pause gets narrower, not wider.
