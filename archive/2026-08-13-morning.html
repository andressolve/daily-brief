Iran / Middle East

Delta since last night: the sharpest direct rhetorical exchange of the cycle overnight; Bab el-Mandeb silence extends to a third session; Gaza saw its first IDF strike in a week; the flat-Hormuz-contract pattern survived its hardest test.

The sharpest public exchange of the cycle, but rhetoric, not action. Trump posted on Truth Social that the US has "total control" of the Strait of Hormuz, calling the blockade a "WALL OF STEEL." Iran's FM Abbas Araghchi hit back on X: "The U.S. has long miscalculated due to intelligence failures. Case in point: The war on Iran," warning of "an even bigger miscalculation" over Hormuz and adding "Worse than fake news is fake intelligence. Be careful." Separately, Basij commander Hossein Taeb said the strait is "under Iran's control" and no vessel transits without Tehran's permission (Al Jazeera, Tasnim).

"Worse than fake news is fake intelligence. Be careful." — Abbas Araghchi, Iranian Foreign Minister, on X (Aug 13)

Read: this is the sharpest direct rhetorical exchange logged since the Aug 10-11 "sweeping demands" story broke — both sides are now making flatly contradictory factual claims (does the US or Iran control the strait) rather than negotiating terms, which reads as entrenchment, not a step toward any deal.

Still no Iranian response to the Bab el-Mandeb attack — now notable for its absence. No IRGC, Foreign Ministry, or state-media statement claiming, praising, condemning, or distancing from the Houthi attack that killed 6 (the first Red Sea shipping fatality in over a year) has surfaced across three consecutive pulls. Tehran's only posture remains its standing general denial of "deep involvement" in Houthi operations — not a fresh statement. Still an open item.

CENTCOM's blockade-enforcement tally is essentially unchanged: ~55 vessels redirected, 3 disabled, 2 boarded since the blockade began. Iran's parliamentary Hormuz bill remains stuck in committee, no floor vote scheduled.

Gaza: first IDF strike after a week-long lull tied to disarmament-roadmap talks. IDF struck Khan Younis, killing a Hamas "company commander" it said was planning attacks; separate reports cite 1 killed/7 wounded in a Khan Younis drone strike plus additional Gaza City casualties (Times of Israel, Haaretz). Netanyahu's rejection of the Board of Peace's 15-point roadmap stands unchanged. A mild escalation signal — worth watching whether it's a one-off or the start of renewed operational tempo.

Cross-market: the flat-Hormuz-contract pattern just survived its hardest test yet. Despite the sharpest rhetorical escalation of the cycle overnight, Polymarket's Hormuz contract sat exactly at 46.5% for a third consecutive session, and oil eased rather than spiked. This is no longer just a kinetic-severity-vs-price-action gap — it now extends to rhetorical severity too. The "markets have priced in a long standoff" read is holding up under repeated, harder tests.

OWL priced $750M in notes (routine refinancing) but unusual call-option volume was also noted — worth attention, still unexplained.
