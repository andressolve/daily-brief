Delta since 7/16 evening Both standing threats materialized overnight — the first reciprocal infrastructure-strike exchange of the cycle.

The US expanded its campaign beyond command/air-defense targets to bridges, energy sites, and a collapsed tower at a port in Bandar Khamir (Hormozgan province), killing at least 7 — Trump's "next week it gets really bad for them" threat executing, not slipping again as it has repeatedly this cycle. Structural read The military/hardline track is now clearly driving events, not the diplomatic track — Iran matched a US infrastructure strike with one of its own on a third-country civilian target, while its own Foreign Ministry simultaneously denied any softening on negotiations.
