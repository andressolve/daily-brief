Structural read

The Axios leak is the most important development since the Al-Arabiya draft. This deal INCLUDES nuclear language — enrichment moratorium, uranium disposal commitment, "never pursue weapons" pledge. This is NOT the purely cosmetic ugly deal we modeled. The nuclear terms are real, even if enforcement is deferred to phase two.

But the Republican Senate opposition is a new constraint we didn't model. Trump's walkback in less than 18 hours shows this pressure is effective. The question is whether Wicker/Cruz represent a blocking coalition or whether Trump can push through despite them. Watch for McConnell, Cotton, or Graham in the next 48 hours. If they join, the deal faces a domestic veto risk.

Revised scenario framing: "Ugly deal" is no longer the right label. This is a "framework deal with nuclear language" — more substance than expected on nuclear, but enforcement deferred. Bear case: deferred enforcement = no enforcement. Bull case: getting enrichment moratorium language into an MOU at all is a major concession from Iran's starting position.
