Delta since last run Ceasefire push widened to four nations with a "middle corridor" concept; Trump hardened rhetoric; new tanker incident; Houthi blockade framed as "effective immediately."

The ceasefire proposal widened overnight from a bilateral Qatar/Pakistan initiative to a four-nation effort adding Egypt and Oman, including a possible "middle corridor" concept for commercial transit — a genuinely broader diplomatic push than yesterday's framing. But neither side has accepted it, and Trump hardened rather than softened overnight: a senior US official says he "hasn't finished retaliating." A tanker was hit by an "unknown projectile" in the Strait (new incident, per UK Maritime Trade Operations), and the Houthi-Saudi blockade is now framed as "effective immediately," with an explicit "eye for an eye" justification tied to the July 13 Sanaa airport strike.

Structural read Wider diplomacy, no less military activity. The three-track pattern — diplomatic/civilian-pragmatist, pragmatic-hawk, IRGC/military — continues running in parallel rather than converging. A broader coalition proposing a deal is not the same as either side being closer to accepting one, and the new tanker incident plus Trump's hardened line argue the military track is, if anything, still live.
