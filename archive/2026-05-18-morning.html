Barakah: A Qualitative Threshold

Three drones targeted the UAE's sole nuclear power plant on May 17. Two were intercepted by air defenses; one struck an electrical generator outside the inner perimeter, causing a fire. No injuries, no radiological release. The UAE called it an "unprovoked terrorist attack." The IAEA chief voiced "grave concern." (The National)

Structural read: Hitting a nuclear facility, even the outer perimeter, crosses a qualitative escalation threshold. Whether Iran or a proxy launched the drones matters less than the signaling: the attack gives the US and Gulf allies political cover for retaliation. Combined with Trump's already heated rhetoric, Barakah is the most dangerous single incident since the May 7-8 fire exchanges.
