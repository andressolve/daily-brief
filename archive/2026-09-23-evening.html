Delta since this morning The 9/22 session is confirmed mediated. No date for the next one. Trump: a deal comes "right after the midterm elections." Pezeshkian's "will not surrender" moved oil. The airline shutdown is being enforced only partially.

Mediated, not face-to-face. Trump said Witkoff and Kushner "had met with mediators," in a session that lasted three hours, with "additional meetings…scheduled in the very near future" (Seoul Economic Daily). Search summaries name Qatari mediators, and Iranian state media confirmed Araghchi's meeting with Witkoff at Witkoff's request (ANI). Israel National News called it indirect from the start.

Structural read. Two things now sit on top of the sequencing gap (Iran: blockade first; US: goodwill first). First, Trump's own timeline is about six weeks out. Second, Iran's head of government drew a no-surrender line on the same afternoon, and oil traded that headline rather than the back-channel. Together they say a channel exists but is not producing Hormuz movement soon. Polymarket priced this before the headlines: the Dec-31 contract has fallen from 24% to 20.5% since the meeting.

Bear case on the "post-midterms" line. It may be campaign expectation-setting that also lowers the pressure to concede now. Or it may be sincere, in which case Iran has a reason to hold its conditions until then. Either way the Oct 31 Hormuz contract at 7.5% is the market's price. Airline shutdown: Turkey will no longer accept Iranian planes and several regional routes are disrupted, but a Mahan flight reportedly still landed in Guangzhou with Chinese routes still selling (single explainer, unverified). Whether Chinese airports and fuelers get secondary-sanctioned is the real test (Kurdistan24, Visaverge).
