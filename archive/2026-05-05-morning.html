Iran / Geopolitics DE-ESCALATION SIGNAL

Delta since last run (May 4 evening): The evening asked "does Trump declare ceasefire broken?" Answer: no. Pentagon explicitly framed Day 1 combat as ceasefire-compatible. Oil falling, equities rising. Evening was too bearish by 6-8pp on Polymarket reads.

The most important development overnight is not what happened in the Strait — it's how the Pentagon framed what happened. Defense Secretary Hegseth and Joint Chiefs Chairman Gen. Caine held a press briefing that created a new analytical category: "ceasefire-compatible combat."

"The ceasefire is not over... [Project Freedom is] a separate and distinct project." — Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, Pentagon briefing, May 5 (WaPo)
"We know full well that the continuation of the status quo is intolerable for America; while we have not even begun yet." — Mohammad Bagher Qalibaf, Speaker/Chief Negotiator, May 5 (Defense News)

Gen. Caine disclosed that Iran has attacked US forces more than 10 times and fired on commercial vessels 9 times since the ceasefire began — and all of it was retroactively categorized as "below the threshold of restarting major combat operations." This means the US military has built a tolerance band for Iranian violence within the ceasefire framework. (CBS)

Trump shifted tone overnight from "blown off the face of the Earth" to Iran "wants to make a deal" — while also warning that efforts to halt shipping passage "will have to be dealt with forcefully." Classic dual-track. (CNN)

UAE response: restrained. Condemned attacks as "treacherous," shifted schools to remote learning, reserves right to respond — but no Article 51 invocation, no formal military request. The "regional war" scenario from yesterday's evening note has not materialized. (The National, Gulf News)

Only 2 merchant ships have transited the new US-guarded route so far. At 2/day vs. pre-crisis 60+/day, Project Freedom is a statement of intent, not a functional reopening. (NPR)

Structural Read: Goldman's "Sloppy Peace" Is Now Operational

Goldman's Jared Cohen predicted that "unless Iran's regime collapses, the Strait will never be open like it was before the war" and the best case was "traffic flowing with Iranian partial control." The Pentagon just operationalized that framework. The US escorts ships through; Iran fires but doesn't sink; both sides call it a ceasefire. This is the new equilibrium until someone breaks it.

Bear case on this framework: Qalibaf's "we have not even begun" is either domestic audience management or a preview of escalation. If Iran sinks a merchant ship, hits another UAE target, or causes a US casualty, the "below threshold" framing collapses instantly. The framework only works if both sides tacitly agree to limited engagement.
