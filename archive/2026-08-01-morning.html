Iran / Middle East

Delta since Friday evening: this is a phase change, not an incremental update — energy-infrastructure strike planning, a US-Israel coordination gap, a reversed Kuwait damage claim, and a formal (disputed) Hormuz closure declaration all landed in one overnight window.

Energy-infrastructure strike planning is the dominant story. US and Israeli officials are reported weighing strikes on Iran's power plants and refineries this weekend, explicitly aimed at degrading grid capacity and cutting electricity to Tehran — a shift from the military/IRGC-asset targeting logged all week toward civilian-infrastructure/economic-strangulation warfare.

A real, two-sided tension, not a unified front: Israeli officials have pushed back, saying they are "unaware of a decision" to proceed. This is either normal operational compartmentalization or a genuine coordination gap between Washington and Jerusalem — the sourcing doesn't resolve which, and this brief won't guess.

Kuwait reversed, not just "addressed." Kuwait's own Ministry of Defense now confirms "material damage" at Ahmad al-Jaber air base from Aug 1 drone activity, plus fresh drone interceptions — directly contradicting Friday evening's CENTCOM statement that "no US aircraft were destroyed or damaged." Two authorities are now on record with contradictory damage assessments across two different incidents (7/31 vs. 8/1) — a live, unresolved conflict, not a settled one. Kuwait's own MOD is the more credible source for damage on its own soil, but it doesn't retroactively confirm Friday's separate claim.

Hormuz, multiple fronts at once: the IRGC claims strikes on two US-escorted tankers transiting the Strait (4 more vessels diverted; CENTCOM says it redirected 24 commercial vessels). Iran's Persian Gulf Strait Authority separately declared transit "not possible" — CENTCOM contradicts this, maintaining ships continue transiting. This is the sharpest version yet of the standing pattern (an Iranian authority declares X, a US authority denies X), but at a higher-stakes level than any prior instance. A separate, unattributed strike hit a tanker near Khasab, Oman (~11:30pm 7/31, engine-room damage) — another unclaimed strike in the same pattern as the Egypt/Damietta strike Iran denied Friday.

Other data points: the Iraq-Turkey pipeline was extended one year, carrying ~750k bpd that bypasses Hormuz entirely — a structural hedge against a full closure, not news of the war itself. Iran has threatened a "comprehensive response" if the energy-infrastructure strikes proceed. The US issued embassy departure warnings for Amman, Jerusalem, and Baghdad — a concrete signal Washington itself is treating this weekend as elevated-risk, independent of any single claim above. No update found on Bahrain follow-up, Saturday's proposed talks, or the Momeni/Mojtaba succession thread — worth flagging explicitly that the diplomatic track looks superseded by this weekend's escalation cluster, not merely stale.
