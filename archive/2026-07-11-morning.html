Iran / Middle East

Delta since 7/10 evening Mojtaba Khamenei's first public statement (vengeance-only), Trump's "1,000 missiles" threat tied to an alleged assassination plot, and hard shipping data confirming Hormuz remains functionally closed.

Mojtaba Khamenei speaks for the first time as Supreme Leader. Posted to Telegram on the occasion of his father's funeral proceedings, the message is unambiguous: avenging his predecessor's killing is "the demand of the nation" and "must certainly" happen. No diplomatic language, no signal he intends to take an active negotiating role. This ends the silence this file has tracked since May 26. Al Jazeera, Britannica.

Trump: "1,000 missiles are locked and loaded and aimed at the Islamic Republic of Iran." Posted to Truth Social, the threat promises to "completely decimate and destroy all areas of Iran" if Tehran acts on an alleged plot to assassinate him — WSJ and other outlets reported this week that Israel shared intelligence on a purported Iranian assassination plan. Treasury separately sanctioned an alleged Iranian financier the same day. This connects to Thursday's "Kill Trump" banners at Khamenei's funeral, already tracked in this file — the assassination-threat exchange is now a distinct, escalating sub-plot running alongside the war itself. CNBC, India TV News.

Hormuz: the paper trail says "progress," the water says otherwise. The IMO Council formally condemned Iran on 7/9 for "continued threats and alleged attacks" on Gulf states' territorial waters — Bahrain, France, Germany, and Saudi Arabia all raised the Strait-transit impact. On the water: zero large vessels transited the coordinated US-escorted route as of the morning of 7/10, and only 34 vessels total transited on 7/9 — 38% of the pre-war daily norm. This is harder, more current evidence than this file's prior tanker-count figures, and it confirms the Strait remains functionally closed despite Friday's Qatar "positive progress" mediator claim. UN News.

Structural read None of today's three threads point toward de-escalation. The throne's first public words are hardline, not conciliatory — a real data point against this file's since-March framing that a civilian negotiating track could eventually route around IRGC hardliners. There is effectively no civilian track at the top anymore; the successor himself speaks in the vengeance register. The Trump-assassination-threat exchange is a new, personal escalation vector layered on top of the existing Gulf-strike pattern. And the shipping data is the cleanest available evidence that mediator-channel talk hasn't yet translated into any physical change on the water — bear case: rhetoric of "progress" is running well ahead of anything observable.

Private Credit

Calibration correction This file's "no material change" framing on Blue Owl since 7/8 missed a real, already-public story that's been sitting there since 7/2.

Per Bloomberg, Semafor, and Investing.com (all dated 7/2 — missed by this file's 7/8, 7/9, and 7/10 dedicated OWL/APO/ARCC/KKR searches): Blue Owl Credit Income Corp (OCIC, ~$34B flagship fund) faced $3.6B in redemption requests for Q2 2026 (18.8% of shares), and Blue Owl Technology Income Corp (OTIC, ~$4.9B software-focused fund) faced $1.1B in requests (38.1% of shares) — both eased modestly from Q1 2026 (OTIC was 40.7% prior quarter), but still extraordinarily high against the industry-standard 5% quarterly cap Blue Owl continues to honor. Separately, UBS flagged elevated concentration risk in private credit's software-sector lending, warning of scenarios where sector-wide default rates could reach ~15% if losses in that concentrated book correlate. Benzinga, Bloomberg.

Reading this honestly

This looks like a structural, ongoing story rather than a new crisis point — "easing but still enormous" (38.1% down from 40.7%) suggests partial overlap with investors who already tried to exit in Q1, not a fresh flight. But a $3.6B/18.8% ask on the flagship fund is a bigger, harder number than anything this file had explicitly quantified for OWL before, and the standing "no material change" framing understated the picture for over a week. Going forward this beat will pull quarterly redemption/NAV disclosures on a rolling basis rather than relying only on press-release-triggered searches. OWL's 7/30 earnings and OBDC's 8/5 AGM are now more loaded catalysts, given this correction, for redemption-stack and cost-of-capital commentary.
