Iran / Yemen Biggest confirmed escalation of the stretch; Flydubai evidence cuts against Trump's claim

Delta since Monday close A declared offensive with a regime-change-adjacent objective replaces a self-reported strike claim. The Saudi side confirms a missile intercept for the first time. The co-pilot's own stated motive (Gaza, not Iran) surfaces.

Saudi-backed Yemeni government forces (the PLC) announced "Operation Yemen Dawn" on Oct 5 — explicitly aimed at retaking Sanaa and ending Houthi control of the country, with roughly 100 Saudi aircraft supporting and hundreds of Houthi targets struck (Al Jazeera, Haaretz). This is a step up in kind from the territorial-claim framing (Dhubab → Mocha) this file tracked through Monday: a named, officially declared operation with a stated maximalist objective, not an incremental advance claim. Separately, on Oct 6, the Saudi-led coalition itself — not just the Houthis — confirmed a live exchange: spokesman Maj.-Gen. This is the first item in this stretch independently confirmed by the Saudi side rather than resting solely on a Houthi or Yemeni-government self-report — a meaningfully stronger evidentiary standing than everything logged through Monday. Yemeni forces' claim to hold Mocha stands a second day, still uncorroborated.

The Flydubai thread sharpened against Trump's claim, not toward it. Reporting surfacing today on the co-pilot's own statements to investigators says he told interrogators he wanted to evade Israeli defenses and crash into Ben Gurion Airport or another building in retaliation for the Gaza war, and that he wished to die a martyr — a first-person motive statement naming Gaza, not Iran, as the stated grievance. Separately, Netanyahu said Israel will formally join the Flydubai investigation while denying any pre-incident warnings were missed. Combined with VP Vance's standing "no conclusive evidence" line, the gap between Trump's unelaborated "I personally do" belief (logged last night) and every other on-record account — his own VP, Israeli intelligence, and now the suspect's own stated motive — has widened, not narrowed, since Monday.
