Iran / Middle East Geopolitics

Delta since this morning: The fuel crisis is the evening's material move — it crossed from "visible shortage" to "regime actively preparing a formal decision," with Khamenei himself now on record backing it. The Oman-corridor dispute and Pakistan channel both held in place. See state/iran.md.

Fuel crisis: the standing highest-value domestic-stability watch item, and it moved materially tonight. Kilometre-long petrol queues are now reported across Tehran, with the daily shortfall reaching ~15M liters. Iran's Executive VP Mohammad-Jafar Ghaempanah stressed the "necessity" of adjusting energy-carrier prices, timing undetermined. More significantly: Khamenei has voiced support for a fuel price-hike decision — "if the heads of the three branches make a decision I will support it" — with the government reportedly giving itself a 10-15 day window to decide and "prepare public opinion" first. Read: this is a genuine escalation from this morning's read, not a continuation at the same level. The 2019 precedent (petrol-price hikes triggering nationwide unrest) is the exact analog this file has been watching for, and this reporting explicitly notes a similar hike-prep pattern preceded January 2026's protests. Genuinely two-sided: Khamenei's public backing also signals the regime believes it can manage the unrest risk this time.

Oman corridor and Pakistan channel: no further movement since this morning. No confirmation or denial on whether the IRGC's claimed corridor deal is finalized. No new Pakistan-channel response from Tehran. Both carry forward unresolved into tomorrow.
