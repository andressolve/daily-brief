Today's real delta is in the market's reaction, not in Iran's stated position. But financial press explicitly tied today's oil selloff to "hopes emerged that the US and Iran would resume diplomatic talks" — the most direct market-level signal yet that Iran's public opening position is being read as a genuine, if early, diplomatic opening rather than pure posturing.

Oil says optimism. Polymarket says: not so fast. Oil fell 2.5-4% today on talks-restart hope — a real, large risk-premium reduction. But Polymarket's Hormuz-normalization contract, this file's preferred calibration instrument, moved only +3 points to 20% Yes — real volume, but nowhere close to pricing an actual resolution. "Talks might resume" and "the shipping war is ongoing" are not mutually exclusive, and today's rally is pricing an early, unconfirmed signal, not a ceasefire.
