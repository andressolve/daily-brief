The leaked draft: what's in it and what's missing

Al-Arabiya published what it called a "near-final" Pakistan-mediated US-Iran deal text. The terms are revealing for what they include and what they omit:

IncludedOmitted
Immediate, comprehensive, unconditional ceasefire (land, sea, air)Enrichment moratorium
Mutual commitment not to target military/civilian/economic infrastructureUranium stockpile provision
Freedom of navigation in Gulf, Hormuz, Gulf of OmanBallistic missile restrictions
Joint monitoring mechanismSanctions architecture (details)
Gradual US sanctions lifting contingent on complianceAny nuclear terms whatsoever
7-day follow-up negotiations on "outstanding issues"—

Structural read: A "final draft" that omits nuclear terms describes a ceasefire framework, not a nuclear settlement. This maps directly to our "ugly deal" scenario — a face-saving cessation of hostilities that papers over the core disagreements. The nuclear can gets kicked to "7-day follow-up negotiations" that could stretch for months or years. It ends the shooting but leaves the underlying tension unresolved.

Bear case on the leaked draft: Rubio's own publicly stated "redlines" — no nuclear weapon, no enriched uranium kept — are not in this text. Either: (a) there's a secret nuclear annex, (b) the US is prepared to accept an interim deal without nuclear terms and negotiate those separately, or (c) this draft gets rejected by the US side precisely because it lacks nuclear provisions. The White House reiterated Friday: "Iran can never possess a nuclear weapon, and they cannot keep their enriched uranium."

The dual-track: negotiate and prepare strikes

While the deal draft leaked, CBS reported the Pentagon is preparing fresh military strikes against Iran. Intelligence community and military personnel cancelled Memorial Day weekend plans. No final decision had been reached as of Friday afternoon.

Trump did not attend his son Don Jr.'s wedding to Bettina Anderson in the Bahamas: "This is not good timing for me. I have a thing called Iran." The couple moved the ceremony from the White House to a private island because of wartime optics.

This is the signal to watch. A president does not miss his eldest son's wedding for diplomatic theater. The strike preparations are either: (a) genuine — strikes happen if Iran doesn't accept the deal over the weekend, or (b) maximum-pressure leverage — the threat of strikes is the tool to force acceptance. Either way, the weekend is a decision point.

Bottom Line
