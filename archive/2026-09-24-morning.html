Private Credit

Delta since last evening The quiet streak ends on the sponsor side. Oracle sent a force majeure notice to Blue Owl's Stack Infrastructure over Project Jupiter. About $18B of bank debt is reported below 90 cents. This is not a BDC-book event.

What happened. Oracle, the anchor tenant of the ~$165B Project Jupiter campus in Doña Ana County, New Mexico, told developer Stack Infrastructure that it may defer payments if the campus misses its 2028 opening. It is not walking away. The trigger is an Energy Transfer gas pipeline, needed to feed the site's Bloom Energy fuel cells, that slipped to February 1, 2027 after the State Land Office twice denied a right-of-way (March, July). About 20 banks lent $18B and the debt is reported trading under 90 cents. Blue Owl committed equity and says the notice "does not change the financial commitments"; Oracle says there is no impact on FY27 guidance. ORCL, OWL and Bloom fell roughly 4–6% (Yahoo Finance / Bloomberg, 24/7 Wall St., AI Weekly). Bloomberg is the origin; CNBC's and Bloomberg's pages returned 403, so lender identities and loan marks are unconfirmed.

Oracle is "attempting to put off payments should the data center dubbed Project Jupiter get derailed and fail to come online in 2028 as planned." Bloomberg, via Yahoo Finance

Structural read. Four points. (1) A notice, not a termination. Oracle is buying optionality. With the pipeline at February 2027 and an opening in 2028 there is slack, so this reads as much like cash management by a BBB- issuer with roughly -$24B of free cash flow as a genuine force majeure event (inference). (2) Who holds the risk. The banks and Blue Owl's equity, not OBDC or ARCC loan books. Transmission to the Q3 prints is through NAV trust and sentiment; OBDC now sits about 24% below its last reported NAV (derived; it was ~20% on 9/8). (3) It revises this file's framing. The $6.5B data-center REIT seeding plan (Bloomberg, 9/4, as logged in the state file; no article URL on file) was treated as a franchise datapoint distinct from the BDC watch. A live tenant-payment clause on a flagship campus weakens that separation. Whether Stack assets are in the REIT seed is unknown. (4) Don't over-attribute to rates. A 5.15% 10Y makes project debt costlier; the stated cause here is permitting.

Bear case: a tenant invoking force majeure is precedent, and other Oracle/Stargate campuses financed the same way carry the same tenant. Bull case: Oracle remains the tenant, the delay is 5–6 months, and Blue Owl reaffirms. Standing watch is unchanged: ARCC late October, OBDC early November. Background: Fitch's record 6.3% private-credit default rate (9/14) is concentrated in smaller issuers and does not map onto this event.

Bottom line / Portfolio implication

The rate path is still setting the tape, but today's new information is a contractual one: Oracle has reserved the right to defer payments to a Blue Owl-developed campus financed with $18B of bank debt now trading under 90. That is the first place the AI-capex financing chain (tenant → developer → banks → sponsor equity) shows a stress point, and the cause is permitting, not rates — which is why NBIS can rally 6% on price increases the same morning ORCL falls 5%. The market is distinguishing who owns scarce capacity from who has promised to pay for capacity that isn't ready.

Zero positions stands, and the honest case on each side is real.
