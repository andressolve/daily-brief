Trump: "1% Chance of Living"

Trump described the ceasefire as being on "massive life support, where the doctor walks in and says, 'Sir, your loved one has approximately a 1% chance of living.'" He called Iran's proposal "garbage." This is a 12-hour escalation from "totally unacceptable" to near-death language. (CNN, CNBC)

Military Options on the Table

Axios reports Trump met with his NSC Monday to discuss next steps. Three options: (a) resume Project Freedom (Hormuz escort convoys), (b) resume bombing — 25% of identified targets still unhit, (c) Israeli Special Forces operation to seize Iran's enriched uranium stockpile — Trump reportedly hesitant because "highly risky." Two US officials say Trump is leaning toward some form of military action to force concessions on Iran's nuclear program. But: officials don't think he'll act before returning from China — creating a ~5-day diplomatic protection window. (Axios)

Ghalibaf Matches Trump

Iran's top negotiator, Parliament Speaker Ghalibaf: "We are prepared for every option. They will be surprised." Armed forces ready to deliver a "lesson-teaching response" to any aggression. Separately told The Hill: "Accept our proposal or American taxpayers will pay for it." This is the pragmatic hawk — not a hardliner, but the man Trump designated as his contact — now matching escalation rhetoric point for point. (Iran International, The Hill)

CEO Delegation: The Commercial Play

17+ business leaders will join Trump in Beijing: Elon Musk (Tesla), Tim Cook (Apple), Larry Fink (BlackRock), Stephen Schwarzman (Blackstone), David Solomon (Goldman Sachs), Jane Fraser (Citigroup), Sanjay Mehrotra (Micron), Cristiano Amon (Qualcomm), Chuck Robbins (Cisco), Kelly Ortberg (Boeing), Meta's Dina Powell McCormick, and more. Jensen Huang (NVIDIA) was NOT invited.

Structural read: This reframes the summit from bilateral diplomacy into Trump's preferred format — transactional deal-making with economic incentives. Micron CEO's inclusion = semiconductor diplomacy. Jensen's exclusion = export control sensitivity (NVDA's China revenue is under active review). BlackRock + Blackstone = capital allocation signals. If Xi offers tariff concessions or AI cooperation in exchange for Iran pressure, the summit could produce unexpected progress on an axis no one's watching. (CNBC, Bloomberg)

Pre-Summit Economic Groundwork

Treasury Secretary Bessent will visit Tokyo May 12 (Japanese PM Takaichi + Finance Minister Katayama), then Seoul May 13 for trade talks with Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng — Xi's trade lieutenant and longtime confidant. This is the economic runway to the summit. Any trade framework emerging from Seoul could shape the Beijing agenda. (Korea Herald, SCMP)
