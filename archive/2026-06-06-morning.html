Wave-2 Bahrain strike — trigger (b) near-tested for the second time

The IRGC fired 7 ballistic missiles and a separate drone wave at the US Fifth Fleet HQ in Bahrain and Ali al-Salem airbase in Kuwait overnight. CENTCOM reports 6 of 7 missiles intercepted by US and Bahraini air defenses; the 7th "did not reach its intended target." No US personnel harmed. CENTCOM called Iran's claim that the Fifth Fleet HQ was damaged "FALSE." Bahrain activated civilian air-raid sirens — the first such alarm of the cycle — directing residents to take shelter. (WaPo Jun 6 · ABC News Jun 6 · USNews Jun 6 · GlobalSecurity Day 99)

This is Wave-2 of the IRGC's stated Fifth Fleet HQ posture conversion. Wave-1 was Jun 3 morning: 3 missiles (all intercepted) + the lateral Kuwait airport drone vector. Wave-2 escalates the volume (3 → 7 ballistic) and adds Bahrain civilian sirens for the first time. Trigger (b) of the four-trigger framework — "Fifth Fleet HQ intercept failure" — is now twice-tested in 96h without breaking. Intercept regime: 13/14 over 4 days.

Bear case: the binary tail is materially tighter than 48h ago. Volume is climbing, intercept rates are statistical, and the 7th missile "did not reach intended target" — read carefully, that's not "we intercepted it," that's "it failed on its own." Wave-3 within 48-72h is the next live test. A single intercept failure on a Bahrain HQ launch — a missile reaching HQ ground or a US casualty at the HQ — would cross trigger (b) and break the Polymarket Dec 31 floor.
