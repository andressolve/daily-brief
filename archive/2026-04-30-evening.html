War Powers: The Institutional Variable

The CENTCOM briefing created optionality but didn't exercise it. The bigger development today was institutional: Hegseth's Senate testimony revealed the administration's strategy for the 60-day deadline. His argument — that the ceasefire "pauses" the constitutional clock — is legally novel and almost certainly wrong. But it tells you what Trump plans to do: ignore it.

The politics matter more than the law. Collins and some Republicans have signaled willingness to join Democrats on requiring authorization. If a resolution passes both chambers, Trump vetoes, and it becomes a political fight about the war's legitimacy. The $25 billion war cost (first official figure) gives opponents ammunition.

Carrier Departure: Force Posture Softening

USS Gerald R. Ford will depart the Middle East in coming days, per CBS News. Force posture: 3→2 carriers. Pentagon says rotation necessity (10-month deployment). But Iran will read the signal: reduced kinetic ceiling, fewer aircraft, fewer cruise missile tubes. If CENTCOM briefing scenarios required 3 carriers, those options are now degraded.

IRGC: Rhetoric Without Action

"Iran will never relinquish its control over the Strait of Hormuz." — Iran Deputy Head of National Security Committee, Al Jazeera

Also claimed Iran has "sufficient missile stockpiles to sustain several years of war." Ghalibaf vowed Hormuz control would ensure "a future without US presence in the Gulf." No softening. No operational escalation either. Pure stalemate.

NVDA Below $200: The Custom Silicon Narrative

The most portfolio-relevant development of the day. NVIDIA dropped 4.4% to $199.98, breaking the psychologically significant $200 level. A Wall Street analyst warned that hyperscaler custom chips pose "significant risk" to NVIDIA's dominance.

The competitive vectors are stacking up:

  • Google: TPU third-party sales announced. Custom silicon with external revenue.
  • Qualcomm: Data center chip deal with unnamed hyperscaler. +15% today.
  • Amazon: Trainium T2/T3/T4 sold out. $50B custom chip business claim.
  • Meta: Multibillion-dollar AWS Graviton deal for agentic AI workloads.

Structural read: the AI infrastructure trade is bifurcating. "Buy the GPU monopoly" is becoming "buy the hyperscaler with the best custom silicon strategy." NVDA Q1 (May 20) is now the most critical earnings report for the AI thesis.
