Iran / Middle East Geopolitics

Delta since this morning: Eisenkot locked in an outright majority as UTJ broke a 20-year alliance with Netanyahu; Herzog is holding the mandate handoff until Sunday. Oil's rebound held through the close.

United Torah Judaism — a Netanyahu ally for two decades — declined to recommend any candidate Thursday, a genuine institutional break rather than a wavering. The Democrats, Yachad, Ra'am, and the Joint List formally united behind Gadi Eisenkot, putting him at 67 of 120 recommendations, an outright majority, against Netanyahu's 46 ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-11-05/ty-article/.premium/eisenkot-secures-parliamentary-majority-clearest-path-to-form-govt/000001a1-ad0b-d041-a5f9-ed2bbb1f0000), [JTA](https://www.jta.org/2026/11/05/israel/eisenkot-has-backing-of-67-seats-but-not-yet-allowed-to-form-government)). President Herzog confirmed the count but will not hand over the mandate until Sunday, Nov 8 — a delay past this morning's expectation of a Thursday/Friday decision. Separately, oil's bounce off Wednesday's crash held through the close rather than round-tripping: Brent ~$96.30 (+1.77%), WTI ~$89.48; no administration figure added anything new today on the war-ending question.

The structural read

This is a bigger and more certain number than this morning's "56, needs 5 more" — Eisenkot now has a mandate-ready majority in hand, and UTJ's institutional break is the more consequential "faction coherence" signal: a two-decade alliance ending is a read on where the ultra-Orthodox bloc thinks power is actually heading, not a tactical wobble. Bear case for Netanyahu: there is no plausible path back to 61 without UTJ, and his "foreign interference" framing — issued before this news broke — now reads as pre-positioning for a mandate he'd already lost on the numbers. Bull case/caution: Herzog hasn't formally awarded the mandate yet, so nothing is final, and Netanyahu's rhetoric could still escalate into a formal challenge once it is. Standing watch: Sunday's mandate decision, any formal challenge from Netanyahu's camp once it's awarded.
