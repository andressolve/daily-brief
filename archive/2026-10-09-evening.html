The structural read

Today is a genuine divergence, not a confirmation: a session with intensifying Saudi-Houthi strikes, an escalating hurricane shut-in, and a still-unrebutted OpenAI revenue question closed with equities up, VIX down, and oil essentially flat. Either the market has concluded none of today's live threads are investable risk, or complacency is building across several uncorrelated threads at once — price action alone can't distinguish those, and that ambiguity is the thing worth carrying into Monday rather than resolving prematurely in either direction.

Bear case: a VIX this low, on a day like today, is exactly the kind of complacency that gets tested hard if any one of the live threads (Saudi response, hurricane landfall, an OpenAI/Microsoft statement) breaks badly over the weekend. Bull case: this is now several consecutive sessions of narrative-driven rallies shrugging off ground-truth escalation without a reversal — at some point that pattern itself, not this file's worry list, is the better forecast.
