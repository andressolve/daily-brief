Iran / Middle East

Delta since this morning: No further kinetic action after the Larak/Jordan/UAE exchange, but Trump raised the stakes by name-checking Kharg Island — Iran's main oil export terminal — as a target. This is a bigger deal than the morning brief's "does it escalate or pause" framing anticipated.

Trump told reporters "we're going to hit them hard" and specifically named Kharg Island as a target if Iran doesn't stand down. Kharg is Iran's primary crude export terminal, handling the large majority of Iran's roughly 1.5-2M bpd of exports. Read: this is qualitatively different from the Larak/Jordan/UAE exchange, which was strikes on military assets — a rocket-launcher position, air bases. A strike on Kharg would be a direct attack on Iran's oil revenue infrastructure, something neither side has done at any point in this cycle, even at its worst. If Trump follows through, this stops being a regional-basing exchange and becomes an attack on Iran's economic lifeline, likely provoking a far larger Iranian response than the proportionate Jordan/UAE retaliation — and a genuine tail risk to global oil supply, not just a war-risk premium, that the market has not priced. If it's rhetoric without follow-through, it's still informative about hardening US posture.

No further kinetic action reported as of Monday's close. The exchange appears to have paused after Iran's Jordan/UAE retaliation, with the escalation risk currently sitting in Trump's rhetoric rather than a demonstrated next move by either side.

Overnight watch for tomorrow's morning brief: does the Kharg threat turn into action, or draw an Iranian rhetorical response specifically? This is now the single highest-value thing to check first thing tomorrow — above the general "does the exchange escalate" framing this morning carried. Fuel-price decree (Pezeshkian's proposed 10,000 tomans/liter) still has no formal order as of Monday close.
