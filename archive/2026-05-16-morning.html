The Enrichment Pivot

This is the structural story. Trump, departing China on Air Force One May 15, told reporters: "20 years is enough. But the level of guarantee from them... it's got to be a real 20 years." (Times of Israel)

This collapses the infinite gap between US and Iranian positions. Previously: permanent ban vs. time-limited suspension = no overlap. Now: 20-year suspension vs. Iran's "shorter period" counteroffer = a quantifiable gap (20 vs. 5-10 years). The negotiation space exists even though both sides reject each other's current numbers.

Bear case on the pivot: Khamenei rejected the new proposal within hours. Called the enrichment demand "excessive and outrageous." Accused Trump of lying. No "reviewing" period — immediate slam. (Iran International) The rejection speed suggests Khamenei/IRGC see the pivot as insufficient, not as a serious opening. Iran wants the TOTAL package (sanctions, reparations, Hormuz sovereignty), not just enrichment flexibility.

Istanbul: E3 Track Activated

Iran is meeting UK, France, and Germany in Istanbul today on nuclear negotiations. Araghchi warned of "irreversible" consequences from UN sanctions snapback. Separately, Secretary Rubio is in Istanbul meeting European national security advisers on Iran and Ukraine. (ABC News)

Structural read: The E3 channel activates as the direct US-Iran track freezes. Iran has engaged seriously with Europeans before (the JCPOA was an E3+3 architecture). But Araghchi's snapback warning signals Iran sees E3 as a threat vector, not a partnership channel. The question is whether E3 can serve as a credible intermediary when the US is not at the table.
