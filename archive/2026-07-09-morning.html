Correction, not just delta

The Mashhad burial (held 7/9) is Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei's own funeral — he was killed in the Feb 28, 2026 opening strike — not a burial of "slain IRGC commanders" as this file's running text had it. IRGC commanders (Qaani, etc.) were mourners, not the deceased. Funeral rhetoric was explicitly vengeance-focused ("we will kill you, Trump" chants, "Kill Trump" placards); the burial was delayed roughly 8 hours, coinciding with US strikes on rail lines into Mashhad. Mojtaba Khamenei, the presumed successor, did not appear — kept away from his own father's funeral over assassination/tracking fears.

"We will kill you, Trump" — chanted by mourners at Khamenei's Mashhad funeral, alongside "Kill Trump" placards (Al Jazeera, 7/9).
Delta since 7/8 evening UAE threat not carried out; retaliation pattern has shifted to Bahrain/Kuwait/Qatar. Hormuz traffic has collapsed to near-zero without a formal closure declaration. Diplomacy is now running indirectly through Qatar.

Iran's threatened "ashes" retaliation against UAE-linked oil/energy infrastructure was not carried out. Overnight retaliation instead continued against Bahrain, Kuwait, and now also Qatar. There was no new Kharg Island action, but US forces struck Iranian coastal cities — Bushehr, Chabahar, Bandar Abbas, Jask — for a second consecutive night, killing at least one person.

The Strait of Hormuz is effectively closed without a formal declaration: only 2 tankers transited early 7/9, versus 13 on 7/8, versus a pre-war average of 125–140 per day. That collapse in traffic is happening quietly, with no announced blockade to anchor a headline — exactly the kind of structural fact that gets lost under louder rhetoric-driven news. Diplomacy is now running indirectly through Qatar: Witkoff and Kushner met Doha officials, but there is no direct US-Iran principal contact.

Bear case: the correction on who died doesn't change the war's trajectory, but it does mean any framing built on "IRGC commanders as the fallen martyrs, Khamenei as an intact command authority" was wrong for over four months of this file's running analysis. That has second-order implications for how much command coherence to assume in Tehran now — a leaderless or contested apex faction is a different actor than a wounded-but-intact one. The retaliation pattern shifting away from UAE could mean either genuine de-escalation toward the Gulf states with basing/overflight exposure, or simply that Iran's operational capacity to hit UAE specifically has been degraded.
