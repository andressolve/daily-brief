Iran / Middle East

Delta since this morning Trump reinstated the naval blockade and announced a 20% Hormuz toll, effective Tuesday 4pm ET. Araghchi responded with mockery-as-rhetorical-claim. New US strikes on Iranian cities accompanied the announcement.

The headline of the day, full stop. Trump reinstated the US naval blockade of Iran and announced a 20% toll on all non-Iranian cargo transiting Hormuz, effective Tuesday 4pm ET. Trump: "We are reinstating THE IRANIAN BLOCKADE, so named because it is only stopping Iran's ships or customers from entering or leaving" — non-Iranian shipping can still transit but pays the toll, with the US recasting itself as "THE GUARDIAN OF THE HORMUZ STRAIT." Humanitarian shipments (food, medical) are still permitted into Iranian ports subject to inspection. CENTCOM confirmed the blockade is not yet in force as of tonight's close — the real enforcement/compliance test is tomorrow, not today.

This is a materially different action than the weekend's claim/counter-claim pattern: it converts Iran's contested "closed" declaration and CENTCOM's "legally open" rebuttal into a US-administered toll system — Washington asserting control of the chokepoint rather than disputing Iran's claim to have shut it. New US strikes on Iranian cities accompanied the announcement — the military track (IRGC unilateral / US strikes) is clearly still running in parallel, not replaced by, today's policy move.

"POTUS is absolutely right... Iran has always been the GUARDIAN of the Strait and will remain so FOREVER. 20% is of course too much. We will be fair." — Iranian FM Abbas Araghchi, responding to Trump's toll proposal, 7/13
Structural read Araghchi's response is rhetorical judo, not a walk-back. He accepts Trump's logic in principle — "whoever provides secure passage should be compensated" — but reasserts Iran's own claim to be the strait's owner and rightful toll-collector. This opens a new economic/propaganda front in the Hormuz fight, distinct from the military one, and gives Iran a way to contest US control without firing a shot. No update found today on the two standing disputed claims (Al Udeid damage, further Araghchi-Oman Muscat safe-passage follow-through) — both flags carry to tomorrow.
