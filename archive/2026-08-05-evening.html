Iran / Middle East

Delta since this morning: the deal narrowed to an Iran-Oman track excluding the IRGC; the public obstacle-narrative flipped from Iran to the US; Netanyahu signaled possible independent Israeli action.

Iran said Wednesday it is in the "final stage" of drafting a joint statement — but explicitly with Oman, not the US; Iranian FM spokesman Baghaei said the statement is ready "if certain third parties do not obstruct this process," widely understood as the US. Iranian semi-official media went further, attributing the delay directly to "US interference and Trump's threats" — inverting the obstacle from Iran (the standing frame all cycle) to the US, in Iran's own telling.

CNN's structural read is the load-bearing new fact: the Iranian negotiating delegation does not include the IRGC, which would have to sign off on the details — the same faction (Vahidi/Rezaei) that controls the IRGC Navy's coercive shipping actions and issued this week's vessel-targeting threat. A senior Gulf official puts the odds of a deal landing by Friday at 50-50.

Separately, Netanyahu said Wednesday Israel will "do whatever is necessary" for its own security and future "regardless of whether the United States is able to reach a deal with Iran" — the first explicit signal in weeks that Israel may act independently of the US-Iran/Oman diplomatic track; watch for whether this stays rhetorical or produces a disconnected Israeli move.
