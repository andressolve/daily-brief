Trump created facts. He didn't sign the MOU.

The morning treated today as a "same-day binary" on the signature. The session resolved as a different binary: Trump posted to Truth Social before the meeting that the blockade "will now be lifted" and "ships… may start the process of 'heading home!'" with three unilateral conditions (no nuclear weapon; immediate open-Hormuz no-tolls; immediate mine removal). Then he convened the Situation Room for two hours and ended it without a final determination — WH officials told reporters they believe the deal is close but "certain matters being debated including the unfreezing of funds for the Iranians."

The structural read is clean: Trump is creating facts on the ground (announcing the blockade lift, winning the narrative) without binding the US contractually (no signed MOU, no released funds, no operational ship movement yet). CENTCOM still has 115 commercial vessels redirected as of May 29. This is politically clever and analytically unstable — the deal-trade is fully priced on an unsigned announcement.

"Ships caught in the Strait due to our amazing and unprecedented Naval Blockade, which will now be lifted, may start the process of 'heading home!'" President Trump, Truth Social, May 29

Iran's read: "fake victory"

Iran's semi-official Fars — the same IRGC-adjacent outlet that mocked the April framework as "Operation Fauxios" — called Trump's characterization "a mix of truth and lies… an attempt to present a fake victory." Per Fars: "The publication of the MoU text is so far removed from Trump's initial fifteen-point demands that even the narratives of Trump's supporters cannot justify it." Separately, FM Araghchi told his Omani counterpart that "reaching a final agreement is contingent upon the cessation of the American side's greedy approach and variable and contradictory stances." So Iran has not confirmed acceptance even after Trump's unilateral move. And critic Dan Shapiro (former US ambassador): the actual MOU "simply opens 60 days of negotiations on disposing of Iran's enriched uranium" — Trump is overselling.

Lebanon: combat zone became kinetic

The IDF advanced ground operations past its existing security line (the "Yellow Line") today, following Thursday's combat-zone declaration. No detail on the depth of advance. Lebanese sources: refugees fleeing north to Sidon. Over 1.2 million displaced since March 2. The structural contradiction with the MOU's reported Lebanon-war-ending clause strengthened through the day — Israel is locking in territory the same week Trump is deciding whether to sign a deal that's supposed to end the war.
