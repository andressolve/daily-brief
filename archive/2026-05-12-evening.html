Iran / Geopolitics Gulf Unified

Delta Since Morning

The Broadest Gulf Response of the War

Saudi Arabia, the GCC Secretariat, and the Arab League Secretary-General each issued separate condemnations of the IRGC's infiltration of Kuwait's Bubiyan Island. The Arab League called it a "dangerous precedent" and cited UNSC Resolution 2817. The GCC called it a "systematic attempt to destabilize regional peace." Saudi Arabia expressed "full solidarity" with Kuwait. (Asharq Al-Awsat, Arab News, QNA)

Structural read: Iran's ground infiltration of a neutral Gulf state achieved what 74 days of Hormuz fighting didn't: it turned every Arab institution against Iran explicitly. The UAE attacks (May 4, 8) started this shift, but they involved a state already in tension with Iran. Kuwait was neutral. That's why this triggered the full institutional stack. This matters for Trump-Xi because it gives Trump a "regional consensus" card to play with Xi: the entire Gulf is aligned against Iran's behavior, not just the US.

Pentagon: $29B and Counting

Comptroller Hurst testified the war has cost $29 billion, up from $25B in late April. Increase from "updated repair and replacement" costs. Senator Murray called the figure "suspiciously low." The fiscal dimension of the war is now entering Congressional debate, which could affect political calculus around escalation vs. deal. (Al Jazeera, PBS)

Lebanon: Hezbollah Draws Line Before DC Talks

Israeli strikes on Kfar Dounine killed 6 today. Save the Children: Israel has killed or injured 4+ children per day on average during the "ceasefire." Hezbollah chief Qassem: weapons are "not part of upcoming negotiations" with Israel, and vowed to turn the battlefield into "hell." Lebanon-Israel direct talks in Washington Thu-Fri. Ceasefire expires May 17. (Al Jazeera)
