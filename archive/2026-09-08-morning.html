Private Credit

Delta since Monday morning: The null streak breaks for real — a ~93% loan markdown this file missed for two sessions. Full detail in state/private-credit.md.

OBDC marked its Loparex loans down ~93% — this is the concrete, name-specific non-accrual event the standing "wait for the Q3 print" thesis has been waiting for, and this file missed it for two sessions. Blue Owl's flagship public BDC slashed its Loparex (specialty paper/liner manufacturer) loan marks from $122.43M at year-end to $8.34M by June, after a planned M&A/recapitalization deal meant to inject fresh equity fell apart, leaving Loparex unable to make a June interest payment on its second-lien debt. OBDC's second-lien Loparex exposure is now marked ~5 cents on the dollar (vs. ~63 cents as of 3/31/26); a first-lien loan marked near par at year-end is now ~22 cents. Bloomberg broke this 9/5; this file's 9/6 and 9/7 morning entries both reported "null streak continues" and missed it. Read: a single ~93% markdown on a $122M position is a real, dollar-quantifiable data point about OBDC's underwriting/valuation practices in a way the standing aggregate 2.8%/0.8% non-accrual rate never was on its own — this sharpens, rather than resolves, the standing Q3-print watch item (late Oct/early Nov), and is worth treating as a leading indicator for whether that print shows an isolated miss or the first of a pattern.
