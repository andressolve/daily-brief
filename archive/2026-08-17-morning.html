Iran / Middle East

Delta since Sunday AM: MOU expired, Trump threatened Oman, Iran denied the Vahidi backchannel story, Lebanon exchange described as deadliest since the June truce.

The June 17 US-Iran Memorandum of Understanding formally expired today with no deal reached. Coverage frames this as "largely symbolic" — the framework was already seen as collapsed — but the timing is the story: it lands the same day Trump threatened to "bomb" Oman if its Hormuz shipping-lane mediation "gets in the way." That threat directly targets the one concrete de-escalation thread logged Sunday morning as genuine progress (the Iran-Oman routing deal). Whether this is negotiating bluster aimed at pressuring Muscat or a real signal that Washington sees Omani mediation as an obstacle rather than a help is the open question — either read changes the Hormuz risk calculus.

Iran formally denied the Axios Vahidi backchannel story reported Sunday: "no message has been sent... nor will any response be given." This is the expected deniability posture for a regime managing factional optics, not a debunk of the original reporting — the open question from Sunday (does a genuine IRGC-hardliner negotiation faction exist) stands uncorroborated either way.

Lebanon coverage now frames the weekend's Israel-Hezbollah exchange as the "deadliest since the June truce," with both sides described as continuing to trade fire. No discrete new Hezbollah retaliatory strike has been confirmed beyond what was already logged Sunday. Bessent's "unprecedented" sanctions package still has not landed, three business days after the "next week" preview — the standing skepticism about whether it materializes continues to compound.

Read: The Hormuz puzzle from Sunday is resolved, not just extended. Sunday's Polymarket drop looked divorced from a seemingly de-escalatory weekend news flow — no new tanker attack, an Iran-Oman routing deal read as mild progress. Monday supplies the missing catalyst: the MOU's expiration and Trump's Oman threat were already baked into Sunday's price action a session ahead of the confirming news. Worth remembering the next time Polymarket diverges from the apparent headline flow — the market may simply be early, not wrong.
