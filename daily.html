The structural read: Tehran and its proxy aren't moving together

The notable new thread tonight: Iranian FM Abbas Araghchi called his Saudi counterpart, Prince Faisal bin Farhan, and explicitly urged a diplomatic solution — warning that "insisting on a military approach... would only further complicate the situation" — and offered to help facilitate Houthi-Saudi dialogue. That's Iran's government positioning itself as mediator on the same day its own proxy executed another strike on the same target. This file isn't resolving whether that's a genuine crack in Tehran-Houthi command-and-control, or cost-free diplomatic cover for a campaign Iran isn't actually restraining — either reading leaves the Houthi campaign itself, the only variable that matters for Hormuz/oil risk, unconstrained.
