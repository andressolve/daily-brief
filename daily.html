Treat the rule's substance as confirmed-by-multiple-independent-secondary-sources, not yet primary-verified. Same-day Asia reaction: Baidu fell to a record low (-5.8%), Alibaba -5%, domestic Chinese chip substitutes (SMIC +6.8%, Hua Hong +8%) rallying.

Confirmed: BIS closed the China AI-chip remote-access loophole, effective 11/16. Multiple trade-compliance outlets report the rule's own text states it was "written by and at the direction of" Treasury and the NSC — an unusual departure from Commerce authoring its own export rules — after Politico reported Treasury Secretary Bessent forced it through Commerce Secretary Lutnick's department, tied to the Megaspeed/Huang espionage allegations. This file's own independent check today could not reach the primary BIS/Federal Register text, so treat the rule as real and multiply-sourced, but not yet primary-verified by this run.

Still unverified: NVIDIA's own reported response. Reporting relayed through an aggregator (citing CNBC sources, not independently fetched) claims NVIDIA created a new board "National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee" and will bar sales to any buyer leasing more than 50% of its GPUs to Chinese firms, allegedly triggered by a 10-day ultimatum from Bessent. A targeted search today found nothing on nvidianews.nvidia.com, investor.nvidia.com, or any SEC filing confirming either claim. Treat this as a lead, not a fact, pending a primary NVIDIA announcement.

Tonight's earnings (after close) are now a quadruple referendum: the standing ~70% FY2028 growth-guide test, any succession-fight commentary from interim CEO Colette Kress (her first call in the role), any direct response to the BIS rule, and confirmation or denial of the reported board committee. The structural read

Today sharpens, rather than resolves, the split this file has carried all week: a real, better-sourced regulatory action narrowing China-adjacent demand, sitting alongside an unresolved governance crisis, with the market's own premarket tape (a broad bounce, a flat Polymarket) reading as calm rather than alarmed.
