AI Ecosystem

Delta overnight: A genuinely new strategic threat to Nvidia's moat, plus a fresh high on the AI-bubble Polymarket contract.

This is a genuinely new, dateable competitive fact — a real, if early-stage, attempt to lower the switching cost away from Nvidia's software stack. VARA remains unresolved, no new development.

The structural read

TorchNeuron is the more structurally important item this morning even though it moved no price today — a credible, well-resourced hyperscaler publicly investing in making Nvidia's software moat optional is a slower-moving risk that compounds over quarters, not days. The AI-bubble-burst contract's continued rise — a fresh high, not a retracement — is the sharper near-term signal. Bear case: a real software-moat threat landing the same week Asia's memory/HBM complex is cratering and Polymarket's own bubble-risk gauge is hitting fresh highs is a worse combination for the AI-infra thesis than any single thread alone. Bull case: CRWV's bonds — the single most stress-tested instrument this file tracks — didn't reprice wider overnight despite the Asia rout, suggesting the market still discriminates between CoreWeave's idiosyncratic story and a sector-wide repricing.
