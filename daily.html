AI / NVIDIA / Markets

Delta since this morning: the catalyst sharpened from "disclosures probe" to "accounting issues," and the market's reaction was the largest of the cycle.

NVDA closed $125.53, down 14.0% on the day, after Reuters reported — citing two people briefed on the matter — that SEC enforcement staff are examining "potential accounting issues" at NVIDIA. This is a materially sharper claim than Tuesday's Wall Street Journal report of a civil probe into disclosures generally; Reuters could not determine when the inquiry began or which accounting practices are under review, and neither the SEC nor NVIDIA confirmed it on record. The session erased roughly $491B of NVIDIA's market value (~$3.52T to ~$3.03T), leaving it down ~48% from its Oct 6 high and ~$2.7T off that peak in six weeks. A fifth investigative/oversight thread also emerged today: Reuters separately reported the DOJ is reviewing NVIDIA's pending $12.9B Hugging Face acquisition — alongside the SEC disclosures probe, this new SEC accounting probe, the DOJ Megaspeed criminal probe, California's DOJ subpoena, and the Meeks/Kamlager-Dove Congressional letter.

AMD closed $424.39, down 13.25% — resolving this morning's open question about Thursday's "unexplained" -7.7% AMD move: in hindsight it was the leading edge of today's broader accounting-probe sympathy selloff, not an isolated gap.
