Iran / Middle East Geopolitics

Delta since last run: Major correction — real escalation across four Gulf fronts over Nov 13-18 that this file's search strategy missed entirely.

Iran's IRGC drone-attacked the US radar system at Bahrain's Sheikh Isa Airbase on Nov 17 — the third such attack in about five days, meaning genuine incidents happened inside what this file had logged as a quiet stretch. CENTCOM and Bahrain say all drones were intercepted with no damage. In response, US fighter jets struck an IRGC missile launch site on Qeshm Island, in self-defense framing from a US official; Iran said the damage was limited.

Overnight into Nov 18, Iran struck a US installation at Kuwait's Ali Al Salem air base — Reuters called it Iran's first reported strike on Kuwait since September. Kuwait and Bahrain both say their air defenses intercepted the attacks with no damage or casualties. Saudi Arabia condemned the attacks on both Gulf states and called for an end to hostilities. A tanker was also struck a second time near Khasab, Oman.

Separately, Qatar's foreign ministry spokesman Majed Al Ansari said at a Nov 17 Doha briefing that the Gulf escalation poses a "grave danger" to the chances of a US-Iran deal, while confirming mediation talks with Iran's side took place that same day — directly contradicting this file's own Nov 17 evening log of "no Qatar-track update." The US blockade (administration officials call it "Operation Economic Outcast") remains in place regardless.

A widely-recirculating "Trump pulls Vance from Iran talks, says no deal needed" story, which surfaced in tonight's search under a Nov 18-dated URL, is actually a stale Nov 12-13 item resurfacing under a reused article ID — not new information, and ruled out as a false lead tonight.
