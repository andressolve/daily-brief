AI Ecosystem

Delta since last night: Dell's backlog print landed, but the governance thread got a new, genuinely political complication.

AI analyst source reviewed: research/2026/2026-11-25-update.md (report date 2026-11-25, as of ~06:00 MST/08:00 ET — the first on-schedule premarket report in several days).

Dell's Q3 FY2027 print (8-K, after Tuesday's close) is the week's cleanest hard demand data point: record $54.9B revenue (+103% YoY), a record $132.3B AI backlog (up from $95B last quarter, after booking $60.2B of new orders), and a raised FY2027 revenue guide of $202.0B (+78% YoY, up from $192B) — a genuine divergence between what one of the sector's largest integrators is reporting booked and what equities are pricing this week.

Against that, the governance thread got more complicated, not less. CBS News and a Wall Street Journal opinion piece report Trump held millions of dollars of NVDA stock while personally pressing the board to reinstate Huang — a disclosed conflict-of-interest question layered on top of an already-unusual reinstatement sequence. Senate Minority Leader Schumer has demanded "a full accounting," and the House Select Committee on China has reportedly widened its inquiry to seek testimony from both Huang and Trump administration officials. Treasury Secretary Bessent's self-set 10-day export-control deadline for NVIDIA's board and outside counsel lands today — no public response has been reported as of this writing. None of this affects the SEC civil disclosure probe, the California AG inquiry, or the Senate Banking hearings already scheduled for Huang (Dec 3) and Lutnick (Dec 10).

A single-sourced, unverified HuggingNews report claims firms linked to "Alice Huang" — the same name already flagged in this file's Nov 18 entry over a 2022 NVIDIA-Alibaba introduction — pledged $270M in AI servers for China-related loans. Flagged as an unconfirmed lead, not a sourced fact — needs primary-source verification before being treated as current.

Read: this is a week where the demand-side evidence (Dell, and NVDA's own Q3 before it) keeps getting stronger while both the equity tape and the governance/political thread get messier — the bull and bear evidence trackers on the computational-abundance thesis are both adding real entries this week, not just one side.
